# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information
are not acceptable in project spaces.

Assume good intent, discuss technical decisions rather than people, and accept
maintainer decisions about scope and moderation. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to
protect the community.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainer
contact options. Do not post personal or security-sensitive information in a
public issue.
